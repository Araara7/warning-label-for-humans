## Entry

> WARNING: This human will insist it is 'five minutes away' while still in bed. Allow 45 minutes and do not trust its ETA.

120 characters. Reads like a real printed safety label ("WARNING:", an instruction, a time allowance) and names a universally recognised human behaviour: claiming to be five minutes away while still in bed. The laugh lands in the last few words — do not trust its ETA.
